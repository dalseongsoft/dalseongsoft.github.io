How it works

On first launch, a 'Quick Notes' notebook walks you through the basics.

STEP 1

Tap +

Choose what to keep: a note, photo, scan, mind map, to-do or alarm.

STEP 2

Put it in a notebook

Group notes by topic into notebooks and shelves so they are easy to find.

STEP 3

Stay on track

Dated items gather on the calendar, and alarms remind you on time.

Your notes stay on your device

Memokan has no sign-up, no login, and no server run by DALSEONG SOFT.

Nothing is collected

Notes, to-dos, events, photos and recordings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them, and the app has no analytics tools.

No tracking

Ads are shown only when you choose to watch one, and the app does not track you with the advertising identifier (IDFA). Payments are handled by the App Store or Google Play.
