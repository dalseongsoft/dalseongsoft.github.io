요약
스케줄칸의 개인 일정, 할 일, 출석 기록, 노트·스캔·녹음, 한 달 기록은 이용자의 기기 안에만 저장되며 개발자에게 전송되지 않습니다. 이용자가 모임 탭에서 '공유 시작하기'에 동의한 경우에만, 모임에서 함께 쓰는 내용(모임 일정표·댓글·투표·준비·정산, 모임에 올린 자료)과 모임에서 보일 이름이 달성소프트가 운영하는 서버에 저장됩니다. 이메일·전화번호는 받지 않습니다. 광고와 사용 통계 도구는 넣지 않았습니다. '설정 › 공유 끄기'를 누르면 서버의 계정과 기록이 지워집니다.

Schedulekan Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026 · Effective: November 28, 2026
App: Schedulekan · Package: app.schedulekan · Developer: DALSEONG SOFT

Summary
Your personal schedule, to-dos, attendance records, notes, scans, recordings and monthly records stay on your device and are never sent to us. Only if you agree to "Start sharing" in the Groups tab, the content you share with a group (group timetable, comments, polls, preparation, expenses, files you post to the group) and the display name you choose are stored on a server operated by DALSEONG SOFT. We do not ask for your email or phone number. The app has no ads and no analytics. Settings › Stop sharing deletes your server account and records.

1. Data stored on the server (only when group sharing is on)

  • A random account ID created without email, your display name and profile color — to show group members who wrote what.
  • Group information (name, dates, place, invite code), members and roles, attendance answers.
  • Group timetable, comments, change proposals and votes, date polls, tasks and packing lists, expense records, notices and read status.
  • Files you post to a group (page images, recordings, text of a note) and access requests/approvals. File passwords are stored only as an irreversible hash.
  • Push notification token (if group notifications are on).
  • Reports (reporter, target, reason) — kept for one year after handling.
  • Server access logs (IP address, time) — for security, kept for the hosting provider's default period.

Data is kept until you stop sharing or delete the group; stopping sharing deletes your account, the groups you created, your posts and files immediately. All tables are restricted so that only members of the same group can read or write them, and all traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). We never sell this data or use it for ads.

2. Processors and international transfer

  • Supabase, Inc. — runs the group server (database, file storage, sign-in). Data is stored in the Seoul region, Republic of Korea (Amazon Web Services).
  • Google LLC (Firebase Cloud Messaging) — delivers push notifications. The notification token and a one-line notification text are sent to Google's servers (including the United States) each time a notification is sent. Turn off Settings › Group notifications to stop this.

3. Data kept only on your device

Personal events and to-dos, places you attend and attendance records, make-up classes, fees, progress, notes, handwriting, scans, photos and recordings not posted to a group, monthly records and patterns, the app lock code (hash only) and settings. Deleting the app deletes them. They leave the device only when you create a backup file or export.

4. Permissions

Camera (scanning), Photos (adding a photo you pick), Microphone (recording, stored on the device), Notifications (reminders and group notifications), Biometrics (unlocking the app lock). Each is requested when first needed; other features work if you decline.

5. Children

Users under 14 cannot use group sharing (confirmed on the start screen). Personal features send no data to the server.

6. Your rights

You can delete your server account and records at any time with Settings › Stop sharing, and edit your display name in the app. For access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing, email us below. Use "Report" in the app to tell us about harmful content.

7. Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the date above.
